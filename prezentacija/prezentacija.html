Distillation

Teaching the draft the target's distribution

Corpus

wikitext-103, train split. 650 653 cleaned Wikipedia paragraphs.

Prefixes

10 000 paragraphs, each a sequence of 256 tokens.

Targets

The 14B model generates 128 tokens onto each, storing its top-50 per position.

What is learned. Not the sampled word, but the whole distribution behind it, because α measures overlap, not agreement on the winner.

Why distil at all

An apprentice who never takes over

Most apprentices train to replace the master one day. Ours trains only to anticipate him: the master still signs every line, he just spends the day nodding instead of writing.

Distillation, as normally used

Train the small model, then ship it instead. Cheaper, and a little worse.

Distillation, as we use it

The small model ships nothing. We only want it to guess the same way more often.

So the goal is not be good at English but agree with this one model. That is why we watch α, not perplexity.

Next steps

Three things we would do next

All three attack c, the side of the equation that actually binds.

  1. •
    Fine-tune with LoRA. Full distillation trains every weight for one run. Adapters touch a fraction of them, so the same budget buys many attempts instead of one.
  2. •
    Swap the draft for DFlash. A diffusion draft emits a whole block of future tokens in a single step instead of writing them one by one. The five draft pulses you saw earlier collapse into one.
  3. •
    Move to EAGLE. Instead of a separate small model, the draft head reuses the target's own hidden states. The guess then costs a fraction of a forward pass rather than a whole one.
α is close to its ceiling. Everything left to win is on the cost side.
Conclusions

What the work established

  1. 01
    Free speed is possible. 2.75× at γ=3 with output identical to standard decoding. Nothing is approximated, so there is no quality argument against using it.
  2. 02
    Guess three tokens ahead. γ=3 is optimal for every draft size we measured. The optimum does not move with the model, so it transfers.
  3. 03
    The smallest draft wins. α saturates (+0.024 then +0.005) while c keeps doubling, so 0.5B beats 1.5B and 3B outright.
  4. 04
    Distillation pays, but little. α 0.905 → 0.93 lifts the result to 2.92×.
